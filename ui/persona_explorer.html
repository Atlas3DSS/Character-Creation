Lyra’s neuron-explorer

This project combines Lyra’s precise neuron exploration with Character Creation’s broader trait, layer, and persona experiments. Lyra developed the neuron sweeps, relative-dose convention, read/write comparisons, and exploratory workflow that motivate this update. This interface and adapter are independently implemented.

The reference recipes below come from the owner-provided transcript of @_lyraaaa_’s October 23, 2026 replies. They target Qwen3.5-4B. They are recorded as reference experiments; their coordinates are not transferred to Qwen3.8-27B.
