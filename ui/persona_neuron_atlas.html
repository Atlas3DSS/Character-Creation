Method

Lens · pushed up / down

logits = lm_head · RMSNorm(± W_down[:, n])

For each neuron we project its down-projection column, and its negation, through the final norm and the unembedding, using the checkpoint's NVFP4 weights in FP32. We keep the top 24 tokens per direction. The colour comes from a weighted vote over the top 12 token kinds. Feeling & character means at least two of the top eight tokens, or the label itself, are words about feelings, desire, temperament, morals or relationships. The label is the most repeated normalized token, otherwise the top token.

What activates it

We use the final-prefill SwiGLU products for all 1,114,112 neurons, from every capture with no steering (or an all-zero program), averaged per distinct prompt. Each authored scenario family maps to one of eight prompt types. A neuron is coloured by the prompt type with the largest standardized mean difference against all other prompts, if that difference is at least 0.8 SD. Otherwise it shows as No clear preference. A prompt type needs at least 60 prompts to colour neurons; smaller ones, including today's “who are you” set, are listed but cannot win, because very short prompts shift the final-token state for formatting reasons alone.

Our evidence

The highest-priority evidence wins: a recorded concept label (activation contrast), a push in a saved recipe (adaptive and preset studies), a hit in the association searches, or a Big Five map candidate.

Setup

Steering convention

h_L ← h_L + c · W_down[:, n]
c = dose · median‖resid_L‖ / ‖W_down[:, n]‖

Limitations

  • The lens is static. It shows what a neuron's output direction favours in the vocabulary. It does not show what the model says when that neuron is pushed. Middle-layer lenses are noisy, and many neurons show as Mixed or Word pieces.
  • Activation is an association. It covers final-prefill activity over authored prompts only. It is not a causal effect, and the prompt types are coarse groupings of the authored families. Long story-continuation prompts differ from chat turns in format as well as content, so part of the Stories colour is likely format.
  • No full single-neuron sweep yet. The reference atlas pushed every neuron of Qwen3-8B and asked “Who are you?”. For this checkpoint we hold “who are you” answers only for saved multi-neuron recipes (shown at the top), not for every neuron. The per-neuron identity sweep has started with the core-emotion neurons; their answers appear in each neuron's panel. On this 27B a single push collapses the reply to nothing near 0.3× at layer 0, so every direction cascades from 1× down to 0.125× and keeps the strongest coherent reply. Coherence is a cheap automatic check (length, special tokens, loops) until John labels the replies.
  • Labels are automatic. No human has checked them.
